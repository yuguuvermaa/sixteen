# Har safhe pe tu (month 16)

A midnight diwan: ten shayaris, nine photos in Mughal arches, a heartbeat, a sealed letter, and a sky with one star for every day since 01:20 am, 25 May 2025.

## Deploy (GitHub Pages)
1. New repo named `sixteen` (the WhatsApp link preview image points to yuguuvermaa.github.io/sixteen/c.jpeg; if you pick another name, change the `og:image` line in index.html).
2. Upload everything in this folder to the repo root: index.html, a.jpeg to i.jpeg, song.mp3, and the whole `fonts` folder (keep it as a folder named `fonts`).
3. Settings, Pages, Deploy from branch, main, / (root). Wait a minute, then open https://yuguuvermaa.github.io/sixteen/

## Edit words
All text she reads is in the `WORDS` object near the bottom of index.html: shayari lines, "what i meant", the letter, the ending lines.

## What she'll experience
1. A clock ticking 01:19:54 to 01:20, then "this is the sixteenth time". Tap "open your book" and the song starts.
2. सोलह in gold leaf, a live counter, and a callback to last month's "Month sixteen. It is set. It repeats."
3. Ten pages (safha १ to १०). Lines surface one by one, then "what i meant" appears.
4. After shayari 5 (dhadkan): she presses and holds a red heart. The screen beats at 72 bpm, her phone vibrates (Android), and it reveals how many times your heart has beaten since 25 May 2025, live.
5. A sealed envelope with the letter.
6. A sky of every day together. Tap any star for its date. "join all our days" pulls them into a heart.
7. Finale plus a live countdown to month seventeen.

Easter egg: open it between 01:20 and 01:29 am IST and the hero notices.
